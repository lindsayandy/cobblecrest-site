Why hire a game master

  • No homework. You do not need to own the books or learn the rules first. Andy brings the materials you need.

  • A private table. Play with only your own group.
  • Relaxed setting. Food and drinks are welcome.
  • A story for your group. Andy shapes the adventure around what your players enjoy.

What Andy brings

  • Andy writes a story with challenges, puzzles and battles.
