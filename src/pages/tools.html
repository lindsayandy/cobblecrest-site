The reading room

The Cobblecrest Archive

Ask the Archivist a question about Cobblecrest and the valley around it. You can ask about people, places or prices. Each answer names the pages it came from. You can open those pages to keep reading.

The Archive also has a shelf of every entry to browse. Its maps of the region and the village have markers. Tap a marker to open the page for that place.

How it answers

  • Answers about the setting come only from the published Cobblecrest guides. Those are the Player's Guide, Eddred the Elder's Regional Guide and the maps.
  • When the guides don't cover a question, the Archivist says so, and you can pass the question to the game master instead.
  • Everyday questions, like the weather or what's cooking at the inn, are answered from a daily almanac of village life. That is friendly color, not official canon. Your own game master decides what is true at your table.
  • Answers are kept suitable for players as young as eight.

The Archivist uses an AI model to write its answers. Each visitor can ask a limited number of questions per day. See the privacy page for what is kept.
