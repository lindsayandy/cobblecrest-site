Monday nights at State of Comics

Play with us

The Cobblecrest game meets on Monday evenings at State of Comics in Plymouth. New and experienced players are both welcome.

When and where

Day and time
Mondays, 6 to 9 p.m.
Place
State of Comics, 575 Forest Ave, Ste. A, Plymouth, MI 48170
Seats
Six players per session, first come, first served
Rules
Fifth edition, with a few house rules

How to sign up

  1. Join the State of Comics game email list. Sign-up links for each month's sessions go out through that list. You can also email Andy at cobblecrestchronicles@gmail.com.
  2. Pick the sessions you want and pay for your seat through the sign-up link.
  3. Make a character on D&D Beyond before your first session. If you are new, email Andy and he will help.
  4. Arrive on time for your session.

Two short videos walk through making a character: D&D Beyond character video, part 1 and part 2.

What a session is like

Each session is an adventure built to finish in one evening, with challenges matched to the party's level. Expect a mix of combat, role play and puzzles in a small group. Come with your dice and your character ready on D&D Beyond. A Player's Handbook helps but is not required. A phone or tablet works fine.

Everyone at the table follows the State of Comics game rules. You can share feedback about any session through the feedback form.

Cancellations and refunds

  • You can move to a different session up to 24 hours before your session starts.
  • For a refund, cancel at least 72 hours before your session.
  • Cancellations within 24 hours, and no-shows, cannot be refunded or moved to another session.

Homeschool groups

Andy also runs regular sessions for homeschool students, with separate groups for younger and older kids. Andy has a background in education. The games give students supervised time together. They also build critical thinking. If your homeschool group or organization would like sessions, email Andy.
