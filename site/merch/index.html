Cobblecrest gear

Merch

Shirts, mugs, stickers and table gear with the Cobblecrest badge. Each item is printed when you order it.

The shop is run by Fourthwall. Fourthwall takes the payment, prints the item and ships it to you. Shipping is charged at checkout, based on the weight of the order.

A few favorites

From the shop

  • Badge Tee A heavyweight cotton tee. Comes in dark colors and light colors.
  • Badge Hoodie A warm pullover with the badge on the chest.
  • Baseball Cap A soft cotton cap. The shop also has a trucker cap.
  • Badge Mug The badge on both sides. Comes in black or white.
  • Badge Sticker Round, matte and water-resistant. Good on a dice case.
  • Tote Bag Organic cotton, with room for books, dice and character sheets.
  • Desk and Play Mat A large soft mat for rolling dice and laying out cards.
  • Graph Paper Notebook Graph paper pages for mapping dungeons and keeping notes.

The shop also carries a polo, a crewneck sweatshirt and a magnet. Questions about an order can go to cobblecrestchronicles@gmail.com.
