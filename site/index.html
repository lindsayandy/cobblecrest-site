Tabletop roleplaying in Plymouth, Michigan

Roll into your role

Andy runs fifth edition fantasy games set in Cobblecrest, a small village in the Forgotten Realms. The village sits in a valley between the Adder Peaks and the Maerthwatch Mountains. Games center on exploration, mystery and smart play.

Three ways in

Find your table

Monday nights

Join the weekly game at State of Comics in Plymouth, Mondays from 6 to 9 p.m. Each session seats six players, and new players are welcome.

How to sign up

Your own group

Hire Andy to run a private game for your friends, family or a single player. He brings the story, the maps and everything else you need.

Prices and booking

Ask the Archivist

The Archivist answers questions about the village, including its people, places, prices and festivals. Every answer comes from the published Cobblecrest guides.

Coming in January

A new campaign begins

The Monday table starts a new campaign on Monday, January 4, 2027. A first session is a good time to join, so watch the State of Comics email list for the January sign-up links.

Later in the winter, plans for the new campaign's sessions will be posted here, free, for other game masters to run at their own tables.
