The setting

The World of Cobblecrest

Cobblecrest is a small village in the Forgotten Realms, as charming as it is dangerous. It sits between the Adder Peaks and the Maerthwatch Mountains, bordered by the Winding River and the old Chondalwood forest.

Meet a few locals

People you may run into

  • Mayor Thomas GreenfieldThe mayor is harried but well-meaning. He speaks for the town council.
  • Elandra ThistlebrewElandra is a gnome brewer. She is a reliable source of rumors and remedies.
  • Jonas and Bethany MillerThe Millers live at the millpond. Everyone in town seems to know them.
  • Marnie GoldweaverMarnie is a trader and fixer. She always has something you didn't know you needed.
