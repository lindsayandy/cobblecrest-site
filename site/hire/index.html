Why hire a game master

  • No homework. You do not need to own the books or learn the rules first. Andy provides everything, or points you to it online.
  • Your schedule. Play at a time that suits you, even if local groups meet when you can't.
  • A private table. Play with your own group, away from a crowded store.
  • Relaxed setting. Food and drinks are welcome.
  • A story for your group. Andy shapes the adventure around what your players enjoy.

What Andy brings

  • A story full of challenges, puzzles and battles.
  • All the materials, including character sheets, maps and rulebooks.
  • Smooth play that works for beginners and veterans alike.
  • A friendly table that encourages creativity and teamwork.
